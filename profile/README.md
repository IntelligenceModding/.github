## About Us

**Intelligence Modding** is a Minecraft modding team and community creating and maintaining a growing collection of mods, tools, and other projects.

Our work ranges from large technology mods and ComputerCraft extensions to smaller utilities, building additions, world-generation features, and vanilla-friendly gameplay expansions.

## Projects

| Project | Description | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| 💾 **Advanced Peripherals** | Expands ComputerCraft with new peripherals, integrations, and automation possibilities. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/AdvancedPeripherals) |
| ⛏️ **Intelligence: Quarry** | A vanilla-styled quarry with filtering, automation, and useful excavation features. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/vanillaquarry) |
| 🧪 **Thorium Reactors** | Technology and power generation centered around thorium reactors, turbines, machines, and resources. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/thoriumreactors) |
| 🔬 **Artemis' Laboratory Blocks** | Connected laboratory building materials and high-tech decorative blocks for modern and futuristic builds. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/LaboratoryBlocks) |
| 🌲 **Artemis' Thin Logs** | Slimmer wood variants for more detailed building and more natural-looking trees. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/ThinLogs) |
| 🍂 **Flora Expansion** | Expands Minecraft's environments with plants, foods, resources, natural details, and survival mechanics. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/FloraExpansion) |
| 🌿 **Baobab Tree** | Adds baobab trees, a complete wood set, roots, litter, fruit, and custom world generation. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/BaobabTree) |
| 🪨 **Climbing Claws** | A survival-friendly climbing tool for traversing walls, ceilings, caves, cliffs, and builds. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/ClimbingClaws) |
| ⚙️ **Omni Wheel** | A client-side radial menu for commands, shortcuts, chat actions, and configurable utilities. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/OmniWheel) |
| 🎟️ **Coupon Codes** | Collectible coupons that provide useful bonuses throughout normal Minecraft gameplay. | [GitHub](https://github.com/IntelligenceModding/CouponCodes) |

Other projects, experiments, development tools, and documentation can also be found throughout our repositories.

## Downloads

Released Intelligence Modding projects are available on **[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/organization/intelligence)** and **CurseForge**.

CurseForge projects are published through the individual profiles of our team members. Their official CurseForge profiles are linked in the **Team** section below.

> Supported Minecraft versions, mod loaders, dependencies, and installation requirements can be found on each project's page or README.

## Community & Support

Our **[Discord server](https://discord.intelligence-modding.de/)** is the main home of the Intelligence Modding community.

You can join to:

- 🔧 Get help with our projects
- 📢 Follow news, releases, and updates
- 👀 See previews and sneak peeks
- 💻 Talk about Minecraft modding, ComputerCraft, Lua, and development
- 📝 Share your own creations
- 💡 Suggest ideas and give feedback
- 💬 Hang out with other members of the community

We also publish project showcases, guides, and other content on **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@intelligencemodding4093)**.
